Privacy Policy for PicklePoint Pro

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Summary: PicklePoint Pro is a 100% offline, privacy-first pickleball scoring and match tracker. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data.

Disclaimer: PicklePoint Pro is an independent offline utility and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or associated with DUPR (Dynamic Universal Pickleball Rating) or any rating organization.

1. Information Collection and Storage

PicklePoint Pro does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). All information entered into the application—including player nicknames, match scores, rally events, and player statistics—is stored solely in local storage on your device.

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, log in, or provide an email address, phone number, or password to use this app.
  • Local Device Storage: All match data resides locally in the application's internal database on your Android phone and temporary outbox cache on Wear OS.
  • No Cloud Servers: We do not operate remote databases or cloud servers that receive or store your match history.

2. Data Synchronization Between Watch and Phone

PicklePoint Pro enables companion synchronization between your Wear OS smartwatch and Android phone using the official Google Wearable Data Layer over local Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. This synchronization occurs strictly peer-to-peer between your paired devices and does not transit through any external third-party server.

3. Third-Party Analytics and Advertising

PicklePoint Pro contains:

  • Zero Advertising: No ads, no banners, and no ad networks (such as Google AdMob).
  • Zero Trackers: No third-party analytics SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer, or Facebook SDK).

4. Permissions Used

The app requests only minimal system permissions essential for core utility functionality:

  • VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during point and fault scoring on your phone and watch.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used briefly to prevent screen dimming during active match scoring sessions so scores remain glancable.
  • No Internet Permission: The application does not declare or request network access permissions.

5. Data Deletion and Export

You maintain full control of your match data at all times:

  • Delete Matches: You can delete any match individually or reset data directly within the app.
  • CSV Export: You can export your match history as a standard CSV file directly to your device storage.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling the app permanently purges all stored match data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Because PicklePoint Pro collects no personal information whatsoever, it does not knowingly collect or solicit data from children of any age.
